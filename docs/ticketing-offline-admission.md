# Offline admission and conflict resolution

Prepare the admission device online for the selected event before opening the door. Preparation downloads a signed, versioned manifest and creates a server-recorded lease scoped to that event and staff identity. PIN preparation lasts at most four hours and never exceeds the PIN expiry; account preparation lasts at most 24 hours. Use one offline lane: devices cannot coordinate duplicates while disconnected.

The device calibrates its clock against preparation time and durably queues the ticket or guest, original admission time, lease token and signed item proof. Preparation proofs cannot be used as ticket QR codes. Automatic replay requires the original staff identity to authenticate again, the recorded time to lie inside both preparation and admission windows, and synchronization within 48 hours after the lease ends. Current ticket version, refund/transfer status, event state, financial holds and PIN revocation still apply. An expired scanner session may authenticate again with the same active PIN; a revoked PIN cannot replay.

Successful replay writes the original admission time and a separate synchronization time. The admission ledger and scan record update transactionally; repeated delivery does not count another admission. A signed lease bounds the recorded time but cannot prove when a dishonest authorized staff member physically admitted a guest.

Managers can use **Import queued scans for manager review** on the device holding the queue when normal replay is unavailable. This validates the prepared evidence and creates a review item; it does not admit someone automatically. In the conflict list, **Confirm admission** requires a note and updates both the admission ledger and the review record. It requires verified preparation, unchanged credentials, an active event and no prior admission. **Reject** preserves the review decision without granting entry. Refunded/transferred tickets, removed or edited guests and cancelled events cannot be restored through this flow. A door person's review note alone does not change the ledger.

Preparation records have a timestamp expiry seven days after the lease ends. Configure Firestore TTL on `ticketingOfflineLeases.expiresAt` when deploying; after record deletion, proof cannot be confirmed. Keep financial and admission audit records under a separate retention policy. Lease tokens and manifests remain private API data and must never be shared with attendees.

Before using offline admission at a real event, rehearse on physical phones: prepare, lose connectivity, scan tickets and guests, reload, reconnect after the admission window, expire/revoke the PIN, and resolve a conflict as a manager. Include battery loss and unavailable camera handling. This local implementation does not establish Safari, installed-app or multi-device offline readiness.
